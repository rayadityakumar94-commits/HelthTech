⚠️ The Problem
Healthcare AI Has a Privacy Crisis
Sending unredacted patient data to external AI APIs violates HIPAA & PIPEDA
⚠️

Regulatory Risk

Healthcare organizations face massive fines for sending PHI to external AI APIs without proper de-identification — violating HIPAA Safe Harbor rules.

🔓

Data Leakage

Patient names, SSNs, dates of birth, medical record numbers and 14 other PHI identifiers are exposed when clinical text reaches cloud AI models.

🐢

Manual Process

Manual redaction is slow, error-prone and not scalable. Clinical teams waste hundreds of hours per month on compliance tasks that AI can automate.

📋

No Audit Trail

Without an automated pipeline, there is no complete audit trail showing which documents were redacted, when, and by whom — a compliance requirement.

👥 User Personas
Who Uses HealthTech?
Three distinct roles with different needs and access levels
👨‍⚕️

Clinical User / Doctor

Primary Role — Most Active User

Uploads raw patient consultation transcripts and clinical notes. Receives perfectly formatted clinical notes with all patient names replaced by pseudonyms. Uses AI Chat Assistant to analyze redacted documents and extract diagnoses without any PHI exposure.

🛡️

Compliance Officer

Oversight & Audit Role

Reviews proxy logs to ensure 100% of outgoing AI payloads have been properly redacted. Downloads compliance reports, monitors PHI detection accuracy, and maintains audit trails required by HIPAA regulations for the organization.

⚙️

Administrator

System Management Role

Manages user accounts, assigns roles, configures AI model settings, sets redaction policies, and monitors system performance. Full access to admin panel with user activation/deactivation controls and system configuration.
