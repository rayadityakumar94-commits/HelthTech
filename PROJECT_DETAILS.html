📋 Project Overview

HealthTech is a full-stack, HIPAA-compliant web application that automatically detects and redacts Protected Health Information (PHI) and Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from clinical documents before they are sent to external AI models. This protects patient privacy and ensures regulatory compliance.

🎯 Executive Problem Statement

As healthcare organizations adopt AI clinical scribes and generative AI tools, sending unredacted patient conversations to external AI APIs violates HIPAA and PIPEDA. HealthTech solves this by acting as a proxy that intercepts clinical text, detects all 18 HIPAA Safe Harbor identifiers, and anonymizes them before the data leaves the organization's secure network.
